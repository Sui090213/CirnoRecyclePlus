<p><i>Cirno Recycle+</i> is a fork of <i>Cirno</i> by flyingr, released under GPL-3.0. It keeps the original app freezer for Android 12+ (cgroup v2): background apps are frozen to stop consuming CPU.</p><p>On top of that it adds a memory reclaimer: frozen apps keep holding RAM and cannot receive onTrimMemory, so their pages are reclaimed directly through the kernel — cgroup v2 <code>memory.reclaim</code> (optionally swapping anonymous pages to zram) plus the system process-compaction interface (MADV_COLD / MADV_PAGEOUT). PSI <code>/proc/pressure/memory</code> is used as the trigger, with cooldown, early stop and multi-round behaviour on high pressure.</p><p>It also ships experimental foreground memory suppression: a <code>memory.high</code> soft cap on the top-app cgroup, cold-page compaction of visible apps, and direct reclaim of the foreground cgroup. Every new feature has a toggle in the app settings screen, and the flashable ZIP auto-installs the companion APK.</p><p>Author: flyingr · Version 1.0.0 · Based on Cirno by Nep-Timeline / Freezer-Team (GPL-3.0). Free, open source, no paid features.</p>
