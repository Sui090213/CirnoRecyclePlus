<p><i>Cirno Recycle+</i> is a fork of <i>Cirno</i> by flyingr. It keeps the original app freezer (Android 12+, cgroup v2) and adds a memory reclaimer: background apps are frozen to stop consuming CPU, and their memory is reclaimed through cgroup v2 <code>memory.reclaim</code> plus process compaction, so frozen apps no longer keep holding RAM.</p><p>Freezing alone only removes CPU load. <i>Cirno Recycle+</i> goes one step further and reclaims the page cache and anonymous pages of background apps directly through the kernel, which also works on frozen processes.</p><p>Author: flyingr · Version 1.0.0 · Based on Cirno by Nep-Timeline (GPL-3.0).</p>
